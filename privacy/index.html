Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Summary

Gridline does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does not do

Gridline has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not use your location.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences (favorite team color, language and time zone) are saved only on your device and are shared with the Gridline widget on the same device. They never leave your iPhone.

Network requests

To show race information, the app downloads public data from the Jolpica API (api.jolpi.ca). These requests contain no personal information.

Children

Gridline is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
