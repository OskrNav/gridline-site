Common questions

Session times look wrong. Gridline uses your device's time zone by default. Open Settings in the app and check Time zone.

How do I add the widget? Long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, search for "Gridline" and choose the medium or large size.

Results are missing. Results appear shortly after each session ends, once the public timing data is published. Pull down on the page to refresh.
